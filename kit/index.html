Long bio, ~150 words

Jatin K Malik is a Principal Engineer at Atlassian and the architect of its agentic interfaces platform in the AI Integrations org, the front door every AI agent uses to reach Jira, Confluence, Trello, Loom and the rest of Atlassian's products through MCP, A2A, CLI, MCP Apps and A2UI. That surface serves roughly 3 million users and more than 50 million tool calls a day (Nov 2026), and he is a contributor to the official atlassian/atlassian-mcp-server. He discussed how it is secured on the Insecure Agents podcast (Ep 67, Nov 2026). Before Atlassian, Jatin led architecture for Uber's Customer Obsession platform, serving billions of events a day, and shipped Adobe Experience Manager features used by Fortune 500 companies. He builds local-first open source, including Vocalinux (offline voice dictation for Linux, covered by ZDNET and Phoronix) and LLMFeeder, and has written at jatinkrmalik.com since 2011. Born in New Delhi, he lives in the San Francisco Bay Area.

Topics I speak on

  • AI agents in production, at enterprise scale. What breaks when agents go from demo to millions of users: gateways, tool design, context, and the operational lessons from 50M+ tool calls a day.
  • Agents are not users: identity and security for MCP. Why agents must not inherit a user's full access, how to gate destructive actions, and where the kill switches go.
  • Distributed systems and data residency. Multi-region architecture, data portability and tenant migration, from Atlassian's 12-region data residency platform and Uber's config systems.
  • Local-first, offline AI. Building useful AI tools that run entirely on the user's machine: the Vocalinux and LLMFeeder story, and why privacy is a feature users can feel.
