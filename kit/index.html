Long bio, ~170 words

Jatin K Malik is a Principal Engineer at Atlassian and the architect of its agentic interfaces platform, in the AI Integrations org. The platform is the gateway every AI agent uses to reach Jira, Confluence, Trello, Loom and the rest of Atlassian's products, over MCP, A2A, CLI, MCP Apps and A2UI. It serves about 3 million users and more than 50 million tool calls a day (Nov 2026). He is a contributor to the official atlassian/atlassian-mcp-server, and he described how Atlassian secures this traffic on the Insecure Agents podcast (Ep 67, Nov 2026), recorded live at AGNTCon and MCPCon North America. Before that he worked on data residency in Atlassian's Enterprise Trust team. Earlier, at Uber, he led architecture for the Customer Obsession platform, which served millions of users and billions of events a day. His first job was at Adobe, on Experience Manager. Outside work he builds local-first open source: Vocalinux, offline voice dictation for Linux (1,000+ GitHub stars, covered by ZDNET and Phoronix), and LLMFeeder. He has written at jatinkrmalik.com since 2011. He grew up in New Delhi and lives in the San Francisco Bay Area.

Topics I speak on

  • AI agents in production, at enterprise scale. What breaks when agents go from demo to millions of users: gateways, tool design, context, and the operational lessons from 50M+ tool calls a day.
  • Agents are not users: identity and security for MCP. Why agents must not inherit a user's full access, how to gate destructive actions, and where the kill switches go.
  • Distributed systems and data residency. Multi-region architecture, data portability and tenant migration, from Atlassian's 12-region data residency platform and Uber's config systems.
  • Local-first, offline AI. Building useful AI tools that run entirely on the user's machine: the Vocalinux and LLMFeeder story, and why privacy is a feature users can feel.
